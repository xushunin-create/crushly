# Crushly Privacy Policy

Effective [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]

This policy explains what Crushly collects, how it's used, who can see it, and how long it's kept. Crushly is operated by [[OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]] ("we", "us"). Crushly is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Southern California.

## The short version

- Your precise location is checked **on your iPhone**. Your coordinates are never sent to us.
- When you choose "Open to matching", our server learns only **which campus space** you chose, on a 60-second lease that your phone renews while you're there.
- Messages are encrypted in transit and at rest but are **not end-to-end encrypted**. We can technically read them.
- People matching in the same space can infer you were there at the same time.
- Deleting your account deletes your data, except a small amount kept for safety (explained below). Deleted data can remain in backups for up to [[BACKUP RETENTION DAYS]] days.

## What we collect

**Account.** Your @usc.edu email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code and to confirm you're a USC student. Your declaration that you're 18 or older, and which versions of these documents you accepted.

**Profile.** What you choose to add: first name, age, pronouns, gender, interests, vibe tags, a prompt answer, what you're looking for, your availability, and photos. Your matching preferences ("Show me" and an age range). Whether your photos have been approved by a moderator.

**Campus space participation.** Only while you've chosen "Open to matching" in a space: the space's identifier, its boundary version, when your session started, and when its lease expires. Your phone also sends the age and reported accuracy of its most recent location fix, so the server can reject stale or implausible claims. It **does not** send coordinates. The session is deleted automatically within about a minute of the lease ending, and right away when you leave the space, go invisible, pause, sign out or delete your account.

**Interests and matches.** Who you expressed interest in during a visit (deleted when either of you leaves the space), which profiles were shown to you during a visit, and your matches.

**Messages.** The messages you send and receive in a match, stored on our servers so both of you can load them. A copy is cached on your device for faster loading.

**Safety.** People you block. Reports you file or that are filed about you, including a snapshot of the reported profile and, when the reporter includes them, copies of up to 50 messages from the conversation ("report evidence").

**Notifications.** Your notification preferences and, if you allow notifications, your device's push token.

**Usage events.** A short, fixed list of events such as "app opened", "profile updated" or "user blocked", with no extra details attached. We deliberately record **no** events about spaces, participation, interests, matches or messages, because together they would form a record of where you were.

**Rate-limit counters.** Counts of recent actions (for example, messages per minute) to prevent abuse. Deleted after two days.

We don't collect your contacts, advertising identifiers or location history, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

## How location works

While the app is open and you've turned on campus matching, your iPhone compares its own location with the outlines of supported campus spaces. That comparison happens on the device. Being inside a space never makes you visible: you choose "Open to matching" for each visit.

While you're open to matching and the app is in the background, iOS shows the location indicator and your phone keeps renewing the lease. If your phone can't confirm you're still in the space, your lease lapses and you become invisible within about a minute. When you leave, it ends right away.

Phones report their own location, so someone using spoofing tools could falsely claim to be in a space.

## Who can see what

**Other users.** People who are open to matching in the same space at the same time may see your public profile (name, age, pronouns, gender, interests, vibe tags, prompt, intention, availability and photos). They never see your location, distance, arrival or departure, whether you're online, or how many people are in a space. No one learns you expressed interest unless they're also interested in you. Your matches can see the messages you send them.

**What others can infer.** If someone sees your profile while matching, or matches with you, they can infer that you were in the same campus space at the same time. A quiet space can also hint at who is or isn't around.

**Us.** Our authorized moderators can access account, profile, report and message data to operate Crushly, review photos and reports, and keep people safe. Access is limited to named people.

**Service providers.** Crushly is hosted on Supabase (database, authentication, file storage and server functions). Push notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service, and notification text never includes message content or locations. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Resend. These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

**Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if required by law, or if we believe in good faith it's necessary to prevent harm, including reporting credible threats to USC Department of Public Safety or law enforcement.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept until |
|---|---|
| Campus space session | About a minute after the lease ends, or immediately when you leave |
| Interests from a visit | Either person leaves the space |
| Account, profile, photos, preferences | You delete your account |
| Messages | You or your match chooses Leave & delete, either of you blocks the other, or either account is deleted |
| Report evidence (message copies) | 90 days after the report |
| Reports | [[REPORT RETENTION, e.g. 2 years after the report is closed]] |
| Usage events | [[ANALYTICS RETENTION]] |
| Deletion records (used to re-apply deletions after a backup restore) | 14 days |

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from Me › Delete account. This deletes your photos and your account, and with them your profile, preferences, sessions, interests, matches and messages.

Two things are kept for safety after deletion: reports you filed (with your identity removed), and reports filed about you (with a snapshot of your profile and your email address as they were when reported, so moderators can recognize a returning account). Deleted data can also remain in backups until they age out. If a backup is ever restored, we re-apply deletions.

Leave & delete removes a conversation for both people. It can't remove screenshots, or copies on a device that's offline.

## Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile, pause matching, go invisible, turn off campus matching, change notification settings, block or report people, and delete your account in the app. You can also turn off location access in iOS Settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example, California), you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, and to have it deleted. Contact us at [[CONTACT EMAIL]] to make a request. We won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## Age

Crushly is only for people 18 and older. Users declare their age when they sign up; this is a declaration, not verification. If we learn that someone under 18 is using Crushly, we'll delete their account. Report a suspected minor in the app.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and server access rules ensure each person can only load what they're allowed to see. Your sign-in session is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and doesn't transfer to other devices. No system is perfectly secure, and messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

## Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update the version and ask you to review and accept the new policy in the app before you continue.
